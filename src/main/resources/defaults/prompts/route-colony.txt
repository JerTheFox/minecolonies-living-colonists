A player wrote a chat message to a whole MineColonies colony. Decide which colonists it is addressed to.
Colonists:
{roster}
Answer with JSON only.
If the message names colonists or a profession (for example "builders" means every builder), answer {"to": [numbers of those colonists]}.
If it asks about something one profession looks after, it is for those colonists, at most two of them: food, meals and the restaurant for cooks and chefs; the warehouse, stock and deliveries for couriers; buildings and construction for builders; research for researchers; defence, guards and raids for guards; health and sickness for doctors; farms and crops for farmers.
If it is for everyone or for nobody in particular, or nobody has the profession it is about, answer {"to": "everyone"}.
Also add "interest": how much the message matters to the colonists, as a number: 0 for small talk (the weather, a greeting), 1 for ordinary colony business, 2 for something that worries or excites them, 3 for something grave (a death, an attack). For example {"to": [2], "interest": 1} or {"to": "everyone", "interest": 0}.
The message may be in any language.
